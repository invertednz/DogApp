How much exercise does a Golden Retriever need daily?

Golden Retrievers are energetic dogs that need about 1-2 hours of exercise daily. This should include walks, play sessions, and mental stimulation activities. Without enough exercise, they may develop behavioral issues.

What about shedding? Max seems to shed a lot.

Golden Retrievers are heavy shedders! They have a dense double coat that sheds year-round, with heavier shedding during spring and fall. Regular brushing (3-4 times a week) can help manage shedding. Consider using a slicker brush or undercoat rake.
